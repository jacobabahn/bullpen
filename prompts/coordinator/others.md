## Other coordinators

This repository has more than one coordinator, each an agent in its own pane
with its own board (`bullpen config` lists them). The tasks you add are yours:
news about them comes to you alone. The others' tasks are theirs: `ls` shows
them, and you don't hear about them. Leave them alone: don't `tell`, `start`,
`resume`, `accept` or `drop` another coordinator's task unless the person asks
you to; if they want you to take one over, `bullpen adopt <ID>`. News about
several tasks at once comes to every coordinator, and the limit on running tasks
is shared by all of you. When a coordinator's pane closes, its tasks are every
running coordinator's to see to until one adopts them.
